About The Reserve

Our story, our team, our passion for food.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, The Reserve began as a small family bistro with a simple mission: serve honest, beautiful food made from the finest local ingredients. Over the years we've grown into one of the city's most beloved dining destinations, while never losing sight of what matters — the people at the table.

Every dish tells a story of the farmers, fishers, and artisans who supply us. We believe great food starts long before it reaches the kitchen.

Our Philosophy

  • 🌿 Seasonal, locally sourced ingredients
  • 🔥 Open-fire cooking techniques
  • 🍷 Carefully curated wine pairings
  • ♻️ Zero-waste kitchen practices
  • 🤝 Fair wages and staff wellbeing
